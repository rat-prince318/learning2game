Nucleus: Stores genetic information and regulates cell growth and activity.
Mitochondrion: Releases energy through aerobic respiration to power cell activity.
Cell membrane: Selectively controls substances entering and leaving the cell.
Ribosome: Reads messenger RNA to build proteins.
Chloroplast: Uses photosynthesis to convert light energy into chemical energy.
Cell wall: Supports and protects the plant cell outside the membrane.
Vacuole: Stores water and solutes and helps maintain turgor pressure.
Cytoplasm: Surrounds organelles and provides a site for many chemical reactions.

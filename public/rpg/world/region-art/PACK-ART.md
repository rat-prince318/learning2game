# Expedition pack item art

The item sheet `pack-items.png` contains 22 tiles from the November 2015 CC0 release of Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup tiles. Original artists and contributors are credited in the [source collection](https://github.com/crawl/tiles). The project distributes a [copy of CC0 1.0](../../battle/art/CC0-1.0.txt).

The cells retain the original 32 × 32 pixels and are packed left to right in eight columns. The roll and flask represent bandages and focus tea in the expedition. Relics share a rune icon and retain their individual names and effects.
